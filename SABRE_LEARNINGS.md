| 2026-07-18T17:54Z | **Sabre is the itinerary system of record, but there is NO list/search endpoint — you must keep your own index.** `get-booking` takes a `confirmationId` and nothing else. You cannot ask "which bookings belong to this event / traveler / PCC." Nothing in the model groups N travellers into one trip — each is an unrelated PNR. **So the local DB is an index + workflow state, not a travel database.** What Sabre gives you free (do not rebuild): segments, `journeys`, fare construction, per-code tax breakdown, baggage allowance *and* excess-bag pricing, ticket coupon status, SSRs, accounting lines, `isCancelable`/`isTicketed`, and `fareRules` with refund/exchange penalties (RRQQNS: $203 refund / $101 exchange — matches reshop). What it does not model: employee↔PNR mapping, the event, tenancy, triage state, voice/call state, policy. **Also two locators per booking:** Sabre PNR `RRQQNS` vs airline locator `CFVLKC` (`flights[].confirmationId`) — quote the *airline's* to the airline. Hotels likewise carry their own. `bookingSignature` + `numberOfUpdates` give optimistic concurrency; store last-seen signature to detect drift. | `sabre-skills` (`get-booking`) |
